README

Practice & "To learn"

Every card sits in a box from 0 to 5, a spaced-repetition schedule. New cards start in box 0, due immediately. To learn on the home screen is simply the count of cards whose due date has already passed - box 0 cards plus anything whose review interval has come back around.

Tapping Practice → Start practicing studies only those due cards by default. If nothing is due, you'll see a "Nothing due right now" screen with a Practice all cards anyway option, which is the only way to study cards that aren't due yet.

Answering Knew it moves a card up one box and pushes its due date out (box intervals: 1, 3, 7, 16, 35 days). Answering Didn't know drops it straight back to box 0, due immediately, and requeues it a few cards later in the same session so you see it again before finishing.

Mastery bar

The mastery bar groups every card into one of three tiers based on its box: New (box 0), Learning (box 1-2), and Mastered (box 3+). Tapping a segment or its legend opens Manage cards pre-filtered to that tier.

Practice modes

Choose Flip Spanish card, Flip Translation card, or Type the Spanish word in Practice setup. Your last choice, along with your deck selection, is remembered for next time.

In Flip Spanish card and Flip Translation card, tap the card to flip it, then swipe right (or tap Knew it) or swipe left (or tap Didn't know) to answer.

In Type the Spanish word, the card shows the translation - type the Spanish word into the field below it, then tap Check (enabled once you've typed something) to flip the card and check your answer, or tap I don't know to reveal it straight away without guessing. Checking ignores case, accent marks, ñ vs n, and ¡¿!? punctuation, so typing without a Spanish keyboard still counts as correct. There's no Knew it / Didn't know here: a correct typed answer counts as knew it, an incorrect one or I don't know counts as didn't know, then tap Next to continue.

Card decks

Decks are tags you can put on a card - a card can belong to any number of decks, or none. Each deck gets a random color as soon as you create it, so its label is easy to spot at a glance in Manage cards.

Practice setup and Manage cards both let you filter by deck, including a "No card deck" bucket for untagged cards. In Practice setup, use the small Select all / Unselect chips to quickly check or clear every deck - Start practicing stays disabled until at least one is selected. Tapping a deck from the Card decks screen jumps straight into Manage cards filtered to it.

Keep practicing & Recently added

Keep practicing lists cards you've recently gotten wrong (failed back down to box 0), most recent first, up to 20. Recently added shows your newest cards instead, but only when nothing is currently struggling - only one of the two lists is shown at a time. Both start collapsed to 3 rows with a "Show more" toggle.

Tap a word in either list to reveal its translation and notes without leaving the home screen.

Export / Import

Export saves a plain-text file in Anki's tab-separated note format (word, translation, notes, deck tags, box, due date, reviewed), so it opens directly in Anki too. Deck memberships are stored as deck::Name tags - a card in several decks gets several tags. Your streak is saved in a couple of header comment lines.

Import reads that same format (older JSON exports still work too). Matching is by word + translation, case-insensitive: a duplicate isn't re-added, but any new deck tags on it are merged in. Missing decks mentioned in tags are created automatically. If it's been a week since your last export, the app will offer to back up on launch.
